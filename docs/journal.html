Each day is recorded when that asset's daily-close update arrives (17:00 New York): the four higher-timeframe cells exactly as the board showed them at the close, and every 6H / 4H / 1H EBP that closed during that trading day (18:00 to 17:00 New York) with whether it was qualified, which timeframes qualified it, and whether it failed.
