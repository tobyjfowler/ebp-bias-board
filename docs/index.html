Each cell is the last closed candle on that timeframe. Bullish: it swept the prior candle's low and closed above the prior open. Bearish: it swept the prior high and closed below the prior open. The level shown is the sweep extreme, which is where the pattern fails.

Higher-timeframe cells are sent from TradingView at each daily close (17:00 New York): daily candles run 18:00 to 17:00, weeks Monday to Friday, calendar months, and 3-day candles counted in threes from the first trading day of each year.

Each higher-timeframe EBP has an equilibrium (EQ), the midpoint of its range. If a lower-timeframe EBP in the opposite direction closes through EQ, the bias flips to that direction (marked EQ) and the violating candle's sweep level becomes the new fail level; a same-direction EBP closing back through EQ reclaims it. A plain close through EQ with no EBP shows as "EQ tested".

Intraday row: the latest 6H, 4H and 1H EBP per asset, sent live from TradingView. A signal is qualified when at least one higher timeframe's bias (including EQ-flipped ones) matches its direction and is not failed; otherwise it is shown faded and no alert is sent.
